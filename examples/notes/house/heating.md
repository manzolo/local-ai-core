# Heating

## Pellet stove

The pellet stove in the living room stops and shows **E5** when the exhaust pipe is blocked. Clean the
burn pot every three days; a full burn pot makes it lock out after about an hour.

The yearly service is booked for the second week of October. Last year it cost 120 euros and included
the chimney sweep.

## Radiators

Bleed the upstairs radiators at the start of the season: the one in the study is always the first to
fill with air.
